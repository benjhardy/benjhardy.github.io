Behind the glass

The agent lab

The front room is for people. The lab behind it is where AI agents work: they set up and run EM and circuit solves through tool servers, queue jobs, log every run, and are scored against benchmarks with known answers. A person reviews before anything is built.

Work with me

Verified AI design loops for RF hardware

I set up agent-driven simulation and design flows inside RF hardware companies, and the checks that show when a result is wrong, so faster design does not mean less trust. In short: an autonomous RF lab of agents, like the one behind the glass, built around your parts and your measurements.

Ben Hardy · PhD, MRI RF hardware · EM simulation application engineer · AI design-agent evaluation

What changed. A simulated design now costs hours, not weeks: open solvers (Gmsh, Palace, openEMS) and agents do the meshing, solving and sweeping. The bottleneck is now trust (is the answer right?) and hardware agreement (does the part match the model?).

  • An EM and circuit pipeline that agents can run headless, validated against your HFSS results and your measurements.
  • Graders and golden cases for each component family, with pass rules, and a second-solver check where it matters.
  • Surrogates with error bars over a stated design space, and a cost per design (unknowns, GB, minutes).
  • A closed loop from spec to measured part, with human review gates where a wrong answer is expensive.
